Moon Mower

Moon Mower is a cosy-comic arcade game about a tiny astronaut mowing a glowing, overgrown moon. Cut paths through lunar grass, rescue trapped spirits, build combos and chase a score before time runs out.

Play on itch.io -> Featured in Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack ->

Automatic mowing, movement-led play

The itch page says mowing happens automatically as the player moves, with boost controls, score chasing, quick restarts and graphics settings. It lists the browser game as released HTML5 and describes it as a few-minute session.

Included in Lofi and Chill Pack

Moon Mower is one of the experiences named in the Lofi and Chill Pack Google Play listing. That Play link is for the collection, while the itch page is the individual browser game page.

Questions about Moon Mower

Is mowing controlled by a separate tool button?

The itch description says mowing is automatic as you move; the player steers, boosts, frees spirits and chases a score.

What does the Android link open?

It opens Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack, which names Moon Mower as one of its experiences; it is not a standalone Moon Mower listing.
