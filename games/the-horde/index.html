The Horde — Ray Tracing Tech Demo

The Horde 1.7 is a short native Vulkan hardware-ray-tracing action showcase for Windows and compatible Android phones. Carry a torch and lantern through a gothic ruin, confront its skeletons and Lich, and follow Kit into a denser forest toward Bellwether.

Download on itch.io -> Source on GitHub -> How does it perform? ->

A fuller journey through the ruin and forest

Version 1.7 adds denser woodland, natural route boundaries, selective fencing and chimney smoke. The playable chapter ends at a temporary road closure before the unfinished village of Bellwether. This remains an experimental technology demo, not a complete game.

Learn, fight and recover

Staged practice teaches the opening combat skills before the Lich calls the skeletons back into live combat. Attack warnings and directional hit feedback help you read the fight. A healing flask has a compact charge display and earned refill basins; Kit walks to the upper-rope basin and explains it according to your remaining charges.

Keep your progress and graphics choices

Use three save slots, encounter Retry and Quit to Main Menu. Hold Attack to sheathe your sword. Resolution can be selected from 25% to 100%, and existing personal graphics choices are retained. Higher-quality options remain available.

Try the benchmark and choose how to share

Open More to find Benchmark. Standard uses a fixed preset and viewport; Custom uses your graphics settings. Both restore your settings afterwards. Keep results locally, send them privately to the developer, or separately opt into the community results website. Public results are unverified and unranked. The scripted course follows rendering speed; normal gameplay does not.

Hardware requirements and testing limits

Windows and Android builds require genuine Vulkan hardware ray tracing. There is no raster, browser, baked, screen-space or software-rendering fallback. Compatibility depends on the exact device and driver. Performance varies by scene and graphics settings, with no sustained mobile frame-rate guarantee.

Questions about The Horde — Ray Tracing Tech Demo

Can I play this in a browser?

No. The Horde is a native Windows and Android demo requiring Vulkan hardware ray tracing.

Will it run on my Android phone?

Your phone and driver must support the required Vulkan hardware-ray-tracing features. Compatibility and performance depend on the exact device, driver, graphics settings and scene workload; a successful install alone is not a compatibility guarantee.

Where can I download The Horde 1.7?

Download the Windows build or signed Android APK from the official itch.io page or the versioned GitHub release.

Where can I see community benchmark results?

Open the community results board to explore submitted hardware and settings. Public results are unverified and unranked; they do not promise a particular frame rate throughout gameplay.
