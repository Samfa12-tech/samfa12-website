How to read these results

One summary per graphics card or phone model. See its average estimated FPS, the range across usable runs and how many runs contributed. More runs usually give a more useful picture. Repeat submissions may come from the same person or device.

Compare matching settings. Resolution and graphics settings can change performance substantially. If a model has several test setups, its most reported setup appears first and the others are available in its details. Different game versions can still be included and are labelled.

Unusual results are kept out of the average. Cleaning starts when at least five runs have usable timings at the same settings. With fewer runs, we keep every usable result and label the small sample. Excluded runs remain available in the details.

These are estimates, not measured gameplay FPS. Each run's estimate uses its typical recorded rendering time. The summary averages those estimates with equal weight per run. Original frame timings and the outlier rule are in the details. All results are unverified community reports, not a leaderboard.
