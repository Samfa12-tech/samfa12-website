Pocket Fish Tank

Pocket Fish Tank is a calm aquarium toy. Decorate a voxel tank, add water and watch fish and other small creatures settle in. You may also see it called Fish Tank on itch.io.

Play on itch.io -> Featured in Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack ->

Create a quiet aquarium

Fill your tank with plants, coral, pebbles, lights and castles, then add fish, crabs, snails and shrimp. There is no score or fail state. The game uses Babylon.js, with WebGPU support where available.

Browser and desktop files, plus a collection listing

Find browser play and Windows, macOS and Linux files on itch.io. The Android link opens Lofi and Chill Pack, which includes a Voxel Fish Tank experience, rather than a standalone Pocket Fish Tank app.

Questions about Pocket Fish Tank

Can the aquarium fail?

No. There's no score and no fail state. It's a quiet aquarium to arrange and watch.

Does the Play link go to a standalone fish tank app?

No. It opens Lofi and Chill Pack, which includes Voxel Fish Tank.
