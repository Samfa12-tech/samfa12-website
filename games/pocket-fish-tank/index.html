Pocket Fish Tank

Pocket Fish Tank is a calm aquarium toy: decorate a voxel tank, add water and watch fish and other small creatures settle in. The itch page calls the product “Fish Tank” in its description, while its page title is “Pocket Fish Tank.”

Play on itch.io -> Featured in Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack ->

Create a quiet aquarium

The itch page describes plants, coral, pebbles, lights and castles, with fish, crabs, snails and shrimp. It says there is no score or fail state and identifies Babylon.js with WebGPU support where available.

Browser and desktop files, plus a collection listing

The itch page lists HTML5, Windows, macOS and Linux. The Android Play destination is Lofi and Chill Pack, whose current listing names a Voxel Fish Tank experience; it is a collection link, not a standalone Play page for this game.

Questions about Pocket Fish Tank

Can the aquarium fail?

The itch page says there is no score and no fail state; it is presented as a calm aquarium toy.

Does the Play link go to a standalone fish tank app?

No. It goes to Lofi and Chill Pack, which lists Voxel Fish Tank as one of the pack experiences.
