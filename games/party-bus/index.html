P.A.R.T.Y. Bus: Wasteland Run

Take a battered yellow bus into the wasteland and get your survivors and supplies back to base. Damage, cargo weight, enemies and fuel all add pressure to the return trip. I made the itch.io version as a prototype. The version on Spawn is the full vision for P.A.R.T.Y. Bus.

Built with Babylon.js

Play on Spawn -> Play the itch.io prototype ->

Scavenge and bring people home

In the v0.9 prototype, drive the Rust Mile, scavenge scrap and fuel, rescue survivors and face hostile encounters. Choose when to turn back, then get everyone home. The bus has visible upgrades, with desktop and mobile touch controls.

The original prototype

The v0.9 build on itch.io is still playable. It captures the early rescue runs and the scramble to make it back to base.

Questions about P.A.R.T.Y. Bus: Wasteland Run

Can I choose when to head back in the itch.io prototype?

Yes. Turn back manually when you're ready, then survive the ride home.

Do I need to download the itch.io prototype?

You can launch the v0.9 prototype right in your browser from its itch.io page.

Updates

2026-11-19 · Spawn public changelog date

Mama Rust brings the bus back

Stranded on foot in the Spawn edition? Call an empty bus and Mama Rust will bring it to you, repairing a wrecked bus along the way. Roadblock crews now react when you approach on foot, and the phone HUD is tidier. The original itch.io prototype is unchanged.
