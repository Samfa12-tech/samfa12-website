Mandible Wars

Mandible Wars is an early playable, portrait-first RTS-lite about ants and termites fighting over a large patch of dirt. Command squads, scout through fog of war, gather nectar, build a colony and capture strongpoints on grid-based battlefields.

Play on itch.io ->

Two modes and competing colonies

The itch page lists Pheromone Conquest, where players capture and hold strongpoints, and Colony War, where the goal is to destroy the opposing colony. It describes phone-oriented controls and desktop support.

A playable build in development

The publisher calls this an early playable build and asks for feedback on controls, balance, bugs, readability and mobile play. Treat current features as prototype details rather than a final release promise.

Questions about Mandible Wars

Which factions can I command?

The current itch description lists Ants and Termites as playable factions.

What are the game modes?

Pheromone Conquest focuses on capturing strongpoints; Colony War focuses on destroying the opposing colony.
