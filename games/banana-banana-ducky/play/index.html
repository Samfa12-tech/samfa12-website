The very important duck handbook

Hugs, not trouble.

1. Catch every duck. Move close and press Space or HUG! Banana Boy hugs, slips, and gives his new friend a red tie. Hold HUG for automatic attempts.

2. Become a duck. Press Q or BE A DUCK to swap between Banana Boy and your last duck. Banana Boy keeps helping! Use Your flock to choose anyone, or return to Banana Boy.

3. Make a plan. Click/tap a tied duck, then a free duck or patch of ground. Tap Auto to let it decide. Commands never remove the helper from your team.

4. Use your trick. Press E or the purple button: Banana Boy dashes; recruited ducks keep their flying, burrowing, cloning, crawling or boxing tricks.

5. Bananas are bonkers. Gold always helps. Stone is a 50/50 gamble. One rainbow banana has a 40% chance per round to appear; it gives a rainbow tie and a choice. A tied duck can choose Freedom and leave the team.

6. Dance! Recruit every real duck to win. Fake clones don't count. Any real duck left when time runs out wins instead. Water and boxing gloves only make you stumble, never die.

Move with WASD / arrows, an iPad or phone joystick, or a gamepad's left stick. Gamepad: A = hug, B = trick, Y = swap, Start = pause. On the Moon, everyone bounces!
