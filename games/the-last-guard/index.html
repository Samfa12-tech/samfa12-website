The Last Guard

The Last Guard is a first-person browser wall-defence survival game. Aim a mounted gun against a fantasy horde, manage heat, call support and bank scrap to improve your chances between stands.

Play on itch.io ->

Hold the wall

The itch page describes mounted-gun combat with overheating and emergency cooling, purchasable weapons, explosive ammunition, defensive structures, artillery support and persistent upgrades between runs.

Browser controls and local progress

The listing describes mouse, keyboard and touch controls, with controller support marked experimental. It says saves are stored locally in the browser and do not require an account.

Questions about The Last Guard

Does the game save progress?

The itch listing says it uses local browser saves and does not require an account.

Are all army sizes ordinary play profiles?

No. The page lists 2k, 6k and 14k as playable army profiles, and labels 28k and 40k as stress profiles.
