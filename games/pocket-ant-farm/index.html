Pocket Ant Farm

Pocket Ant Farm is a relaxing voxel ant-colony simulation in a glass-sided dirt slab. Watch ants dig, explore and forage; drop food, suggest digging areas and make the soil translucent to see the tunnels.

Play on itch.io -> Featured in Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack ->

An observation toy in an early build

The itch page describes a small colony that starts with one ant and can grow by adding workers. It says the prototype emphasizes atmosphere and observation rather than strict win-or-loss play, and recommends portrait mode on phone, tablet or desktop browser.

Included in Lofi and Chill Pack

The Google Play listing names Pocket Ant Farm as one experience in Lofi and Chill Pack. The Play destination is the collection listing, not an individual Android Pocket Ant Farm app.

Questions about Pocket Ant Farm

Can I control where ants dig?

The itch description says you can tap the farm to place dig hints and drop food; ants dig and explore within the simulation.

Is Pocket Ant Farm a standalone Play Store app?

The current Play link is for Lofi and Chill Pack, which lists Pocket Ant Farm as one of its experiences.
