Spin Vector

Spin Vector is a neon twin-stick arcade shooter about piloting an upgradeable vector drone through themed arenas. Fight through waves, collect credits for persistent upgrades and face boss encounters; the Android listing also describes post-game Gauntlet and Adventure modes.

Play on itch.io -> View on Google Play -> View on Wavedash ->

Arena combat and upgrades

The Google Play listing describes ten themed arenas with twelve waves each, mini-bosses on waves six and nine, and a main boss on wave twelve. Credits can be spent on persistent ship upgrades. It also describes an unlockable Gauntlet and a post-game Adventure hub after clearing the arenas.

Adaptive music

The Play and itch descriptions say the game has adaptive music; the itch page says it is powered by the Pocket Chordsmith engine. The current Play listing describes the Android release; an older itch excerpt refers to closed testing.
