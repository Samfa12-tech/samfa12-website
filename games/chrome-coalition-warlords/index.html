Chrome Coalition

The AI took control.
The Chrome Coalition is fighting back.

Battle across a neon-lit canyon where the battlefield won’t stay the same for long. Blast through walls, carve tunnels through solid rock and find a new way into the enemy base. Just remember: your opponents can do the same.

Make your own path

Chrome Coalition is an in-development browser FPS inspired by the chaotic multiplayer battles and destructible environments of the PS2 era. Choose a side in the fight between the human resistance and the machines, grab weapons around the map and make your own path through the battlefield.

Planned multiplayer

The initial target is 4v4 online multiplayer, with bots filling empty spots.

Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch and Capture the Flag are planned as development progresses.
