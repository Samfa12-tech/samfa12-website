Red Line: Parkour and Surf

Run the rooftops, find your line and carry that speed onto the ramps. Red Line: Parkour and Surf is a first-person time-trial game set in a white city, with red and yellow markings guiding you through the route.

Built with Three.js

Play on Spawn ->

From rooftops to surf ramps

Sprint, vault and climb across the city before dropping onto steep ramps. Lean into the slope to surf at speed, then try to carry your momentum through the next section.

Chase a better run

Race your own best time and the room-record ghost, and climb the leaderboard. A fall sends you back to the last checkpoint so you can retry the section rather than lose the whole run.

Play on Spawn

Play in your browser on Spawn. The pause menu includes a reduced-motion switch that turns off camera roll, speed zoom, wind lines and landing shake.

Questions about Red Line: Parkour and Surf

What happens if I fall?

You return to the last checkpoint to try again.

Is there a reduced-motion option?

Yes. The pause-menu setting disables camera roll, speed zoom, wind lines and landing shake.
