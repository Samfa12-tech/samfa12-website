Reckless Dentist

Reckless Dentist is a short browser score-attack game about treating cartoon teeth at speed. Spot the problem and drag the right tool into place while the clinic grows more chaotic.

Play on itch.io ->

Fast dental microgames

The itch page describes cleaning, filling and extraction, with Shift Mode and Patient Mode. It supports mouse and touch drag-and-drop and stores a local best score in the browser.

Cartoon comedy

The publisher describes the game as deliberately silly, with cartoon mouths and synthetic comedy sound effects; the page says it contains no blood or realism.

Questions about Reckless Dentist

How do the controls work?

Use mouse or touch to select a tool and drag it to the affected tooth.

Does the browser save a score?

The itch page says a local best score is saved in the browser.
