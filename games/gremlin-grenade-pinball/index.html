Gremlin Grenade Pinball

Gremlin Grenade Pinball is a prototype that combines pinball with a gremlin horde: keep a live grenade moving with the flippers, hit traps and try to stop gremlins advancing down the board. Its itch page labels the game as in development.

Play on itch.io ->

Prototype features and plans

The itch listing describes a spring launcher, flippers, bumpers, trap targets and gremlins that move down the board. It separately labels features and five themed boards as planned or in development; these should not be presented as completed content.

Questions about Gremlin Grenade Pinball

Are all five listed boards complete?

The itch page presents five boards as planned and says the game is currently a prototype in development. It does not establish that all five are complete.
