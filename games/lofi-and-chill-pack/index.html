Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack

Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack is an Android collection of calm, low-pressure scenes with soft lofi music and voxel-style visuals. Its Play listing describes fish, ants, birds, a train set, a koi pond and Moon Mower as experiences in the collection.

Get it on Google Play ->

A collection of pocket worlds

The listing names Voxel Fish Tank, Pocket Ant Farm, Pocket Aviary, Pocket Train Set, Pocket Koi Pond and Moon Mower. It describes mobile-first scenes with graphics settings and short, relaxed sessions; the listing calls the collection growing, and the selection may change.

Pack listing versus individual browser games

The Google Play destination is the collection app. Pocket Fish Tank, Pocket Ant Farm and Moon Mower also have their own itch browser pages; that Play link is for the collection, not a standalone Android listing for each game.

Questions about Samfa12's Lofi and Chill Pack

What scenes are named in the current Play listing?

It names Voxel Fish Tank, Pocket Ant Farm, Pocket Aviary, Pocket Train Set, Pocket Koi Pond and Moon Mower. The listing describes the collection as growing, so that list may change.

Is there a separate Play app for each named scene?

The catalogue's Play destination is one Lofi and Chill Pack collection listing. Some individual experiences also have separate itch browser pages; the pack URL is not an individual game listing.
