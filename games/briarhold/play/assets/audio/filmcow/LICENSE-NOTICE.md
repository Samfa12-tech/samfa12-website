# FilmCow Recorded SFX

The runtime files in `runtime/` are compressed derivatives of original sound
effects recorded by **FilmCow**.

Use of the supplied FilmCow bank in Briarhold was explicitly authorised by the
project owner on 8 August 2026. No separate licence document was present beside
the supplied archive, so these files are not represented as CC0, public-domain,
or reusable outside Briarhold. The FilmCow recordings and derivatives remain
excluded from any broader source-code licence that may apply to Briarhold.

Exact source filenames, hashes, runtime hashes, and derivative settings are
recorded in `provenance.json`.
