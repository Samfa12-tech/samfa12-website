About the game

Defend the hold. Survive the night.

Briarhold is a first-person action-defence roguelite and spiritual sequel to The Last Guard. Take the role of a Warden, explore a haunted fortress, and keep the Briar Host away from the Heart Gate across seven escalating nights.

Between attacks, repair the old stone and prepare your next stand. Then return to the walls with your arbalest, Sunfire, and Runebolt. Play solo first, with an optional two-Warden co-op direction planned for the future.

  • Seven-night fortress defence campaign.
  • Repairable defences and build breaks between waves.
  • First-person combat with three distinct weapons.
  • Original adaptive score: The Green Remembers.
