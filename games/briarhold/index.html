About the game

Defend the hold. Survive the night.

Briarhold is a first-person action-defence roguelite and spiritual sequel to The Last Guard. Take the role of a Warden, explore a haunted fortress, and keep the Briar Host away from the Heart Gate across seven escalating nights.

Between attacks, repair the old stone and prepare your next stand. Then return to the walls with your arbalest, Sunfire, and Runebolt. Play solo or host an experimental two-Warden online co-op session.

  • Seven-night fortress defence campaign.
  • Repairable defences and build breaks between waves.
  • First-person combat with three distinct weapons.
  • Original adaptive score: The Green Remembers.

New in Alpha.101: fixes the recurring Mason repair, Strengthen and New Run freeze, and improves close-body auto-fire, Wicker pursuit, enemy facing and lighting, woodland appearance and co-op recovery and firing. Optional report diagnostics can now include game progress and recent errors to help reproduce bugs. Night 5's intermittent freeze, two-phone and cross-network co-op, and sustained performance remain open test areas. Both co-op players should update to Alpha.101.

Using Briarhold

Briarhold is a first-person action-defence roguelite with browser and Android testing builds. Prepare defences between attacks, fight the Briar Host and experiment with multiplayer co-op, which remains an experimental feature.

Defend the fortress

Briarhold is described as a first-person action-defence roguelite, with repairable defences prepared between attacks. The public Alpha.101 notes focus on combat and fortress fixes; browser play and Android testing use separate destinations.

Co-op is experimental

The Alpha.101 release notes say co-op recovery, input, avatar cleanup and firing presentation were improved. They also state that the intermittent Night 5 freeze remains under investigation and that two-phone, cross-network play and sustained performance remain active test areas.
