Reef Sweep

Dive into Lantern Bay, hunt invasive lionfish and give the reef room to recover. Explore coral gardens, a sunken wreck and an old outfall, then bring your catch home and put your earnings towards better diving gear. Reef Sweep is a first-person browser game in development.

Play Reef Sweep ->

Explore Lantern Bay

Start in the warm shallows of Sunlit Gardens, then unlock the stronger currents of Tideglass Passage. Follow schools of fish through the reef, explore the deeper trench and visit the Lantern wreck. Seaweed sways with the current, your regulator keeps you breathing, and bubbles rise towards the surface. You can swim up for a look at the coast, though surfacing won't refill your tank.

Hunt lionfish and restore the reef

Get close, settle your aim, hold to draw the pole spear and release to strike. Hold Interact to secure the fish in your collection tube before moving on. Lionfish spines hurt and drain your air, so give them room. Between hunts, clear abandoned fishing gear, plant nursery-grown coral and survey field stations. Take on restaurant orders and conservation contracts, and return to a reef that remembers your work.

Better gear and night dives

Spend your earnings on fins, a better spear, more air and a battery-powered scooter. Nine equipment types have five upgrade levels each, giving you more room to explore as your diving business grows. Upgrade your regulator to level 2, fit a head torch and unlock Tideglass Passage to try a night dive. Keep an eye out for the reef shark. High graphics adds shadows across the reef, including those cast by your torch; reload when prompted to apply the full detail.

Prepare your catch

Back at the harbour, try the optional filleting activity. Time three careful cuts to earn up to 30% more when you sell your fish. You can skip it and go straight to deciding what to sell, donate or serve as meals.

Play your way

Use WASD and the mouse on desktop, or swim with the touch stick and drag the scene to look around. On touch, hold and drag Draw to aim, then release to strike. Portrait and landscape layouts are available. Controllers are supported too, with a USB Backbone preset and a calibration guide in Settings if your buttons or sticks need remapping. When mouse capture is unavailable, you can still look around by moving the mouse over the game.

Music that follows the dive

The original score, “Room for Life,” uses sounds adapted from Pocket Chordsmith. Its layers change with the dive, wildlife and danger.

Questions about Reef Sweep

Where can I play Reef Sweep?

Play it here in your browser using the Play Reef Sweep button. The game is still in development.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress stays in the browser you're using. There are three save slots, plus export and import options if you'd like a backup or want to move your save. Saves don't automatically carry between devices, browsers or a downloaded copy of the game.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Touch controls support portrait and landscape play, and there's a fullscreen button. Landscape play has been tested in Android Chrome. Other phone setups, including portrait, fullscreen and iPhone/Safari, still need more testing.

Is the game finished?

It's an early playable build. Both dive sites, equipment upgrades, night dives and filleting are available, but the artwork and balance are still being worked on. Expect a few rough edges.
