Reef Sweep

Reef Sweep is a first-person diving and reef-conservation game set in Lantern Bay. Take your skiff out, hunt invasive lionfish, help the reef recover and bring your catch home.

Play an early demo now. The full game is in development, with release timing to be announced.

Play the demo -> Play the demo on Wavedash ->

Head out. Dive in. Make room for life.

Choose a dive site, follow the reef and find your next lionfish. Move close, steady your aim and draw the pole spear. Secure each catch before moving on, and watch your air so you can make it back to the boat.

What you can play in the demo

Explore five dive sites, clear abandoned fishing gear, plant coral and record surveys. Bring your catch back to prepare, sell or donate, then spend your earnings on better diving gear. Local contracts, reef recovery and night dives give you reasons to head out again.

Questions about Reef Sweep

Where can I play Reef Sweep?

Choose Play the demo to play the current browser demo here, or Play the demo on Wavedash. The full game is in development, with release timing to be announced.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress stays in the browser you're using. There are three save slots, plus export and import options if you'd like a backup or want to move your save. Saves don't automatically carry between devices, browsers or a downloaded copy of the game.

Can I play on my phone?

The demo has touch controls for portrait and landscape play, plus keyboard/mouse and controller support. Phone performance varies; compatibility with every Android and iPhone browser has not been fully verified. On iPhone, check Silent Mode and media volume if there is no sound.

Is the game finished?

No. This is an early playable demo, separate from the full game in development. The current demo includes five dive sites, equipment upgrades, night dives, catch preparation and a working dive watch. Artwork and balance are still being worked on. Release timing for the full game will be announced.

Detailed demo features and controls

Explore Lantern Bay in the current demo

Drive your diving skiff across the bay, follow the navigation chart and slow beside a gold buoy to choose a dive. A pilot and diver ride aboard the Blender-authored skiff, with swells, buoyancy, wake and bow spray. At a dive buoy, the diver checks their gear and rolls backward into the water before the chosen reef loads. Skip the entry when you want to get straight to the dive; Steady camera skips the sequence too. Underneath are five authored sites: Sunlit Gardens, Tideglass Passage, Mangrove Reach, Azure Cenote and Rusted Harbour. Their ledges, roots, limestone basins and old structures offer different routes. Playable reefs retain clear water, with distance haze beyond their boundaries.

Hunt lionfish and restore the reef

The remodeled lionfish have striped bodies, broad fans and long dorsal spines. Their defensive display flares as you approach and relaxes when you retreat. Identify a lionfish, draw the pole spear and release a careful strike. The actual fish stays on the spear until you guide it into the catch tube. Spear a second before stashing for a Shishkabob moment in the existing automatic-photo highlights. Larger fish have restaurant value; smaller targets still count towards eradication. Remove abandoned gear, plant coral and survey the reef. Marlow, the named grouper, makes his home in Sunlit Gardens. Ordinary sea stars, lobsters and large sponges bring quieter corners to life.

A reason to come back

Permanent reef work stays done. Find five authored keepsake charms and clip a favourite beneath the spear grip. Later progression introduces marked crown-of-thorns outbreaks: position the treatment tool, release its safety and apply the fictional treatment for a conservation bounty. Every third game day still brings the local Sweep event, and automatically captured highlights build your in-game audience.

Better gear and night dives

Spend your earnings on fins, a better spear, more air and a battery-powered scooter. Nine equipment types have five upgrade levels each, giving you more room to explore as your diving business grows. Later equipment service gets cheaper as you gain field experience. The scooter’s motor rises with your thrust and quietens when you stow it. Upgrade your regulator to level 2. Head torches become available on game day 4 with six conservation reputation; fit one and unlock Tideglass Passage to try a night dive. Keep an eye out for the reef shark. High graphics adds shadows across the reef, including those cast by your torch; reload when prompted to apply the full detail.

Check your dive watch

Raise your wrist to check air, depth, time, secured catch, battery and return reserve. Press V, tap Watch or use your controller mapping. Portrait retains the dive-stat display; landscape frees the view for the watch. Breaching leaves draining droplets on the goggles. A forceful wildlife impact can partly flood the mask; clear it with Interact or the accessible button at a small air cost.

Prepare your catch

At the harbour, try optional 3D scaling and three guided fillet cuts, then cook citrus pan fish or harbour coconut stew. Filleting adds a quality bonus to fish sold; a prepared meal offsets a small extra expense when a fish is kept. Allocate the catch and select up to two automatic highlight images before settling. Towing and boat fuel appear separately on the receipt and depend on the trip distances.

Play your way

Use WASD and mouse on desktop, or steer and swim with the touch stick. Hold and drag Draw to aim, then release to strike. Portrait and landscape use distinct, comfortable action buttons. Controllers retain the Backbone preset and calibration guide. New saves start on High graphics; existing choices remain saved. Sustained slow gameplay can offer a lower preset, with Apply and Not now. Fullscreen appears where supported; Wavedash provides its own control.

Music that follows the dive

The original score, “Room for Life,” uses sounds adapted from Pocket Chordsmith. Music starts after your first menu interaction and continues through boat travel and diving. The boat motor responds to speed, while the score changes with wildlife and danger. Sound settings stay saved, including mute; pausing or leaving the tab quietens the appropriate sounds.
