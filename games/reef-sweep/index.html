Reef Sweep

Dive for invasive lionfish, help restore the reefs around Lantern Bay, then bring your catch home, prepare it and get to know the coastal community. Play the full game free on Wavedash.

Head out. Dive in. Make room for life.

Choose a dive site, follow the reef and find your next lionfish. Move close, steady your aim and draw the pole spear. Secure each catch before moving on, and watch your air so you can make it back to the boat.

Updates

2026-10-28 · Samfa12.com campaign availability announcement date (Australia/Sydney)

Full campaign arrives on Wavedash

Reef Sweep’s full Lantern Bay campaign is available on Wavedash. At the time of the announcement, an earlier website demo was also available; that demo has since been retired and its old play link now leads to Wavedash.

2026-10-17 · Samfa12.com deployment date (UTC)

v0.5.0-preview.3 — crew, water entry and lionfish display

Menu and boat audio now start after interaction, with a speed-responsive engine. A visible pilot and diver accompany boat trips, followed by a short gear-check and backward-roll entry at the selected reef. Remodeled lionfish flare their fins and spines independently as the diver approaches, then relax on retreat. Driving starts behind the skiff with mouse, touch and controller camera control; the close cinematic view is limited to water entry. Existing saves and gameplay damage rules are preserved. Sam confirmed the local audio, entry, lionfish display and corrected driving camera. Physical-phone performance remains unverified.

2026-10-15 · Samfa12.com deployment date (UTC)

v0.5 expedition preview and portrait refinements

The v0.5.0-preview.2 browser build brings boat travel around Lantern Bay, keepsake charms, optional catch preparation and cooking, and later crown-of-thorns conservation work. Portrait refinements make the boat chart compact and lower the catch tube between collections. The game remains in development; physical phone acceptance of the new features, sustained performance and natural campaign pacing remain unverified.
