Drink

Drink is a short, top-down narrative game about alcohol dependence, avoidance and the beginning of accountability. Its fixed story follows a person through a small town where drinking brings short-term relief and consequences accumulate.

Play on itch.io -> View on Steam -> View on Google Play ->

A contained story with one ending

The itch page describes a finite game with sparse dialogue, constrained money and morally weighted choices. It says the ending is fixed and focused on accountability, not recovery; the subject matter includes alcohol dependence, denial, avoidance and shame.

The game and its related works

Drink is the game. Sober is described as a novel set after the game, and Drink (Original Soundtrack) is a separate album. These are related works, not alternate editions of the game.

Questions about Drink

What should players know about the subject matter?

The game depicts alcohol use and deals with dependence, denial, avoidance, shame and accountability. The itch page says the subject matter may be uncomfortable for some players; Google Play labels its rating for use of alcohol.

What platforms are available?

The itch listing offers HTML5 and Windows builds. Google Play lists the Android game, and the listing says the mobile and Steam versions are fully featured with controller support. These platform statements describe their respective store listings.
