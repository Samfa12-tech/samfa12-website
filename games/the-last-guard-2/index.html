The Last Guard 2: Hold the Wall

40,000 orcs. One wall. A city behind it. You’re one of a handful of guards still standing on the city’s final line. Then the horn blows, and the valley lights up with torches.

Free to play in your browser. WebGPU is required, and a desktop PC is recommended.

Play on Wavedash ->

Pick your targets

Take the mounted heavy gun. Powder runners charge the wall with lit fuses, brutes close on the gate, and jetpack raiders come in from above. Fire a rocket into a packed column or call down lightning when the horde bunches up.

Spend your salvage

Take your salvage to the Arsenal and upgrade your kit. Those upgrades carry into later runs, and you can bring surviving defenders into the fight.

Questions about The Last Guard 2: Hold the Wall

How big is the siege?

Choose 2,000, 10,000 or 40,000 orcs when you deploy. Each size has its own fastest-clear leaderboard, and Arsenal upgrades can help you beat your time.
