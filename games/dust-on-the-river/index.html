Dust on the River

Dust on the River is an Australian historical life game set in a fictional north-east Victorian river town in the 1890s. You arrive under a new name, take station work, ride, fish and pan for gold while a past tied to bushranger Red Jack starts to catch up.

Play on itch.io -> View on Google Play -> View or purchase novelisation on Amazon ->

A frontier story in Victoria

The story is set in Crossing, a fictional town inspired by Victorian goldfields, railway towns and high-country settlements. It follows a former bushranger trying to make a new life through ordinary work and relationships with townspeople.

Work, ride and explore

The Android listing describes station jobs, moving livestock, riding between Crossing and nearby stations, fishing, gold panning, eating, resting and saving progress. The itch page describes its HTML5 browser build as unfinished and still in development, so platform status differs by build.
