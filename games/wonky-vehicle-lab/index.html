Wonky Vehicle Lab

Wonky Vehicle Lab is a prototype vehicle-building sandbox for drawing odd machines and testing them on a bumpy physics track. Sketch parts, connect them with glue or welds, then drive and see whether the design reaches the finish.

Play on itch.io ->

Draw, build and test

The itch page describes drawing frames, struts, wheels and seats; joining parts; and sharing inventions with WVL1 build codes. It lists HTML5, keyboard, mouse and touchscreen support, and a MIT code license.

A prototype with usability work remaining

The page labels the project a prototype. A publisher reply to player feedback says mobile scaling was poor at the time; current mobile usability has not been verified.

Questions about Wonky Vehicle Lab

Can I share a vehicle design?

Yes. The page describes copying and pasting WVL1 build codes to share vehicle designs.
