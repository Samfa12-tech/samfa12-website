Night Shift at Possum's Cafe

Night Shift at Possum's Café is a cosy pixel-art café game about serving animal customers as a possum barista. Prepare simple drinks and treats, keep orders moving through quiet moments and rushes, and gradually make the neighbourhood café busier and more inviting.

Play on itch.io -> View on Google Play ->

A small café and short shifts

The game focuses on one Australian-inspired night café, simple order-making and replayable sessions. Its listed menu includes flat whites, tea, hot chocolate, croissants and cake; the tone favours a gentle atmosphere over complex management systems.

One game across two store names

The itch page and Google Play description identify the same possum-barista café game. Google Play's store title is “Possum Café: Cosy Barista Game,” while the product description calls it “Night Shift at Possum's Café.” The Play listing says it contains ads.

Questions about Night Shift at Possum's Cafe

What do you do in the game?

You serve animal customers by preparing drinks and treats, including flat whites, tea, hot chocolate, pastries and cake. Google Play also describes small upgrades and timed service as part of play.

Is the Android listing ad-free?

No. The Google Play listing currently labels the app “Contains ads.” Store details can change, so check Google Play for current information.
