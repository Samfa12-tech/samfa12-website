Ship Blockade

Ship Blockade is a lane-based arcade tower-defence game about stopping ships from breaking through a shipping lane. Place guns, mines, missile batteries and blocker boats, adapting as faster and more heavily armoured vessels arrive.

Play on itch.io -> Featured in Samfa12's TD Pack ->

Build while the pressure rises

The itch page says defences can be placed during a wave, with a build delay, and towers can be upgraded between waves. It also describes the game as an early prototype focused on gameplay and balance, although the listing status says Released.

Included in the Android pack

The Play link points to Samfa12 Tower Defense Pack, a collection. It does not identify a standalone Android Ship Blockade app.

Questions about Ship Blockade

Is Ship Blockade a finished game?

The itch listing status is Released, but its own Notes section calls it an early prototype focused on gameplay and balance. The page preserves both details rather than treating the status label as proof of completion.

Can I get it from Google Play by itself?

The current Play destination is the Samfa12 Tower Defense Pack collection; no standalone listing was verified.
