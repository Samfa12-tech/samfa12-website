How it works

  1. You play. Wavedash calculates each enrolled game’s share of its monthly Creator Fund from eligible playtime and reviews the report.
  2. I confirm November’s allocation. Once Wavedash’s final November figures are known, I’ll record the four-game breakdown and total here.
  3. I make the donation. I’ll donate the full allocation to the Great Barrier Reef Foundation and cover any payment or conversion fees separately. Personal tax and development costs won’t be deducted.
  4. I publish the record. I’ll add the amount, date, currency/conversion details and a redacted receipt after the donation is made.

Read Wavedash’s official Creator Fund rules.

The payout threshold won’t hold this up.

If Wavedash’s threshold or payout timing delays the money, I’ll advance the donation personally once the final November allocation is known.

The eventual payout reimburses that advance. It won’t be counted as a second donation.

A few questions

Does each play donate a fixed amount?

No. The fund uses eligible playtime across enrolled games, and Wavedash reviews its monthly figures. There’s no guaranteed amount per session. Please just play normally—don’t automate play or artificially extend sessions.

What happens if earnings are below the payout threshold?

The pledge still applies. Once the final November allocation is known, I’ll advance the full donation personally if the threshold or payout timing delays Wavedash’s payment.

When will the figures and receipt appear?

After Wavedash finalises November’s allocation, I’ll publish the breakdown and make the donation. The donation date and redacted receipt will follow the actual donation. No donation has been recorded yet.

Can I donate through this page?

This page doesn’t collect player donations. You can learn about the Foundation and its own ways to help on its official website.

Is this an official partnership?

Reef for Good is an independent Samfa12 initiative. It isn’t a partnership with, or endorsement by, the Great Barrier Reef Foundation or Wavedash.
