Reporting periods and sources

Wavedash: Verified Wavedash Audience / This period exports captured 16 November 2026, 08:52-09:06 AEDT. The final day is partial. This is a rolling Last 30 days snapshot, not November campaign totals or lifetime totals. Players are distinct within each game only; overlapping reports are not added.

Spawn: All-time figures reported by Spawn for the listed editions. Provider cut-off timezone is unconfirmed; some counters may include live activity. Players are per game, not unique people across games. No revenue or personal data is included.

Spawn snapshots refresh on Mondays when source access is available. Capture dates show the latest successful refresh. Per-game player counts must not be added together as unique people.

Storefront figures retain their original lifetime counters. Withdrawn itch.io listings are omitted from release cards; historical lifetime totals are preserved.
