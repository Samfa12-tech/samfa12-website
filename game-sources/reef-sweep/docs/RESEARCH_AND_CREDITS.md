## Educational boundaries

The fictional setting is Caribbean/western Atlantic, where the invasive-lionfish premise belongs. The game does not claim lionfish should be removed from their native range. Removal, debris management and nursery-supported coral work have distinct roles. No feeding mechanic encourages conditioning predators to associate divers with food.

Air, rescue, underwater currents, restoration timing and wildlife warnings are deliberately simplified. Do not use game metres/bar, animal timing or reserve estimates to plan a real dive. Real coral work requires appropriate training, permissions and local expertise; planting in this game is an abstraction at designated nursery plots.
