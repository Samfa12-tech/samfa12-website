Backwater

By Samfa12

Backwater is an Australian post-apocalyptic novel about a water carrier crossing a water-scarce landscape. Its story follows the strain of survival, grief and exhaustion, and the difficult choices that can let isolated communities cooperate.

A journey shaped by water

The author describes Backwater as a story about water, survival, exhaustion, grief and carrying on after the world has broken. The book is the source for a separate early game prototype; the prototype is a different project and should not be mistaken for the novel.
