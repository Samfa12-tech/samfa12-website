ToKnight 2: The Fire Beneath

By Samfa12

ToKnight 2: The Fire Beneath is the second book in the Jason Proud series. Jason, Kiera and Angus find an ancient door beneath their school library and a wounded dragon below it, while a new student takes a dangerous interest in Jason's family name.

The Jason Proud series

The author's series description follows an ordinary Australian Year Six student whose family legacy brings spectral ancestors and dragons into school life. It presents courage as responsibility and kindness rather than a search for glory.

A middle-grade fantasy sequel

The author recommends the book for readers around ages 8–12, especially children who enjoy dragons, secret magic and school adventures. The itch.io page is a companion page; the novel is directed to Amazon rather than offered as an itch.io download.

Questions about ToKnight 2: The Fire Beneath

Where does this book fit in the Jason Proud series?

The author identifies The Fire Beneath as the second book in the Jason Proud series. ToKnight is the preceding Jason Proud adventure.

Who might enjoy it?

The author recommends it for readers around ages 8–12 who like dragons, secret magic, school-based adventures and funny fantasy with heart.
