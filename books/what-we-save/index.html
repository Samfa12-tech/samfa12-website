The Broken Road Trilogy — Book 3 · Coming soon — 1 Dec 2026

What We Save

By Samfa 12

What We Save concludes The Broken Road trilogy. Tomas Parker returns to a threatened Auster with refugees, wounded soldiers and grain, as the city faces another choice about who and what its defences protect.

Pre-order on Amazon ->

The closing Auster campaign

The Amazon synopsis presents the final book as an adult low-fantasy story of siege warfare, engineering, political conflict and morally difficult decisions. It brings Parker back to the capital and follows the pressure on a city whose survival depends on more than its walls.

Third in The Broken Road

Amazon marks this Kindle edition as Book 3 of 3 and lists 1 December 2026 as its publication date. At the 23 September 2026 review, its buybox showed a preorder with that scheduled Kindle delivery date.

Questions about What We Save
