Free to a Good Home

By Samfa12

Free to a Good Home is a contemporary Australian young-adult mystery-drama about fifteen-year-old Tom, who meets Jonah during a family holiday at a caravan park. Jonah is a watchful boy with no clear story about who is meant to be caring for him, and Tom finds it increasingly difficult to ignore the gaps around him.

Buy on Amazon ->

A mystery about noticing

Tom is already frustrated by the holiday and the responsibilities around his younger brother Max when Jonah appears. The author describes the novel as a story about family, responsibility, silence and the fragile hope of being safe.

Questions about Free to a Good Home

What is the story about?

During a family holiday, Tom notices Jonah, a boy who seems to be appearing in places where adults should be looking after him. The story follows Tom's growing concern and the question of what responsibility asks of him.
