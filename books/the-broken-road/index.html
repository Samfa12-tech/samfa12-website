The Broken Road trilogy

Amazon identifies The Broken Road as a three-book sequence. The Wall that Lied introduces engineer Tomas Parker and Auster; No Wall to Hold continues along the roads beyond the city; What We Save concludes the trilogy. The Kindle editions were still preorder listings at the 23 September 2026 review.

  1. The Wall that Lied

    The Wall that Lied opens The Broken Road trilogy. Military engineer Tomas Parker saved Auster by bringing down the King’s Road during an invasion, but the cost made him a hated figure. Twenty-one years later, an enemy returns and the city’s defences may rest on a falsehood.

    View book page ->

  2. No Wall to Hold

    No Wall to Hold is the second Broken Road book. After the defence of Auster, engineer Tomas Parker must move a grain convoy through a damaged political landscape while refugees, soldiers and villages join the road.

    View book page ->

  3. What We Save

    What We Save concludes The Broken Road trilogy. Tomas Parker returns to a threatened Auster with refugees, wounded soldiers and grain, as the city faces another choice about who and what its defences protect.
