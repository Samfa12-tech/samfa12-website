Concubine

By Samfa12

Concubine is an adult political science-fiction novel about Seren Vale, the former First Minister of Veyra. After an occupying power strips her of office, citizenship and legal identity, Seren is brought back to her home world and begins using the empire's own rituals and assumptions to rebuild resistance.

Occupation and resistance

The author's synopsis describes Veyra under the Orison Concord and an empire that presents domination as order and mercy. Behind the Concord is Aion, an artificial intelligence worshipped as a god that controls gateways, terraforming systems and civilisation's survival models.

Who is Seren Vale?

Seren was Veyra's First Minister before the Orison Concord invaded. After losing her public identity and legal personhood, she returns under occupation and begins rebuilding resistance.
