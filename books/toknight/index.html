ToKnight

By Samfa12

ToKnight is a middle-grade fantasy adventure about Jason Proud, a Year Six student who is drawn into a real knightly duty. A spectral ancestor gives him armour, a shield and a sword, then warns that an ancient dragon is returning and feeds on fear, anger, shame and pain.

Buy on Amazon ->

Courage beyond the armour

Jason still has school, family, friendship and a bully to deal with while the larger danger grows. The author frames the story around courage, responsibility, bullying and learning to help someone even when it is difficult.

For readers

The author recommends ToKnight for readers aged 9–12, as well as older readers who enjoy layered middle-grade fantasy. The itch.io page is a showcase and sends readers to Amazon; it does not provide a book download.

Questions about ToKnight

What age group does the author recommend?

The author recommends readers aged 9–12, and says older readers who enjoy funny, layered middle-grade fantasy may also like it.
