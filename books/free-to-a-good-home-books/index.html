Free to a Good Home and A Place of His Own

The author describes A Place of His Own as a sequel to Free to a Good Home. These two books follow related characters at different points in their lives; the current public sources do not establish a formal series name.

  1. Free to a Good Home

    Free to a Good Home is a contemporary Australian young-adult mystery-drama about fifteen-year-old Tom, who meets Jonah during a family holiday at a caravan park. Jonah is a watchful boy with no clear story about who is meant to be caring for him, and Tom finds it increasingly difficult to ignore the gaps around him.

    View book page ->

  2. A Place of His Own

    A Place of His Own is a quiet Australian young-adult novel and a sequel to Free to a Good Home. Seven years after Jonah was found at a caravan park, he is nearly seventeen and his living arrangement has become uncertain; the Walsh family must consider how to help without deciding his future for him.
