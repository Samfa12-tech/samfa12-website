Sober

By Samfa12

Sober is a novel that continues the narrative world of the game Drink six months after its ending. Told through alternating perspectives, it follows Drink and Sober as they live separate, structured lives in the same city while memories of the past surface in ordinary moments.

Buy on Amazon ->

Two lives moving forward

Drink lives in a rented room and works a routine job; Sober works long shifts in a pharmacy. The author describes the book's tone as restrained and observational, with minimal dialogue and small moments rather than dramatic speeches.

Reading formats

The author's Amazon availability announcement lists Kindle, paperback and hardcover editions, and says the itch.io page offers the book free or for a small donation. Check the retailer for current edition availability.

Questions about Sober

How is the novel related to the game Drink?

The author calls it a direct continuation set six months after the game. It includes the character Drink and introduces Sober's perspective; the two stories then move forward separately.
