The Wall that Lied

By Samfa 12

The Wall that Lied opens The Broken Road trilogy. Military engineer Tomas Parker saved Auster by bringing down the King’s Road during an invasion, but the cost made him a hated figure. Twenty-one years later, an enemy returns and the city’s defences may rest on a falsehood.

Pre-order on Amazon ->

Siege and engineering

The Amazon description frames the first book as a character-driven military fantasy about siege warfare, engineering, political pressure and the human cost of hard decisions. Parker is called back to defend Auster while the past and his estranged family remain unresolved.

First in The Broken Road

The Amazon listing marks this Kindle edition Book 1 of 3 in The Broken Road and shows a 1 October 2026 publication date. As reviewed on 23 September 2026, the store offers a preorder; the date is a scheduled Kindle delivery, not evidence of a completed publication.

Questions about The Wall that Lied

Where does this book sit in the trilogy?

Amazon labels The Wall That Lied as Book 1 of 3 in The Broken Road. No Wall to Hold and What We Save are listed as Books 2 and 3.
