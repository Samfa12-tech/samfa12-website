Article 18

By Samfa12

Article 18 is an adult comic-noir mystery novella about Austin Crowd, a private investigator whose instinct for patterns often leads him toward the wrong explanation. A sealed packet draws him into a tangle of missing records, political manoeuvring, polite threats and questions people would rather he stopped asking.

Download on itch.io -> Read ebook on Google Play -> Listen on Google Play ->

A case that keeps changing shape

Austin works through offices, receptions and back channels with help from Jules, a connector who is not impressed by his methods. The author describes the novella as comic noir: its mystery turns on Austin's confident misreadings as much as on the people trying to silence him.
