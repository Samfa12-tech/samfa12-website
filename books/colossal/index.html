Colossal

By Samfa12

Colossal is an adult historical tragedy set in ancient Rome. Aurelius is an enslaved gladiator whose victories make him valuable to the people who own him; behind the champion's public image, he studies the arena and begins turning his fame into a chance to resist.

Survival under the spectacle

The author's synopsis follows Aurelius as he protects a young recruit, looks for a way out and faces Rome's demand for submission. The novel's stated concerns include ownership, survival, revolt and the attempt to keep hold of one's identity.
