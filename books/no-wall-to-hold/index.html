The Broken Road Trilogy — Book 2 · Coming soon — 1 Nov 2026

No Wall to Hold

By Samfa 12

No Wall to Hold is the second Broken Road book. After the defence of Auster, engineer Tomas Parker must move a grain convoy through a damaged political landscape while refugees, soldiers and villages join the road.

Pre-order on Amazon ->

A defence that has to move

The Amazon description shifts the campaign from a city wall to roads, bridges and the people depending on them. Supplies, authority and promises all become harder to manage as the column grows; the story is presented as adult low-fantasy military fiction about logistics and consequences.

Second in The Broken Road

Amazon marks the Kindle edition as Book 2 of 3 and currently lists 1 November 2026 as its publication date. At the 23 September 2026 review, the store displayed a preorder with that scheduled Kindle delivery date.

Questions about No Wall to Hold

Should I read The Wall that Lied first?

Amazon numbers The Wall That Lied as Book 1 and No Wall to Hold as Book 2 of The Broken Road. Reading the first book gives the preceding Auster events and Parker’s earlier choice.

Has the Kindle edition been released?

At the 23 September 2026 review, Amazon Australia showed a preorder scheduled for 1 November 2026. Check the listing for current availability.
