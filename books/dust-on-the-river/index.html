Dust on the River

By Samfa 12

Dust on the River is an Australian historical novel based on the Samfa12 videogame. In a fictional north-east Victorian river town in the early 1890s, a newcomer hopes steady work will let him leave a bushranger past behind.

Buy on Amazon ->

A new name in Crossing

Caleb Reed arrives by train and begins to earn trust through stock work and practical help. The Amazon description places him among river country, goldfield rumours and people who know more about his past than he wants to admit. This page keeps the later revelations in the listing for readers who want them.

The book and the game

Amazon identifies this novel as based on the Dust on the River videogame. They are separate works: the book is a historical novel sold in Kindle and paperback editions on the listing, while the game has its own browser and Android destinations. The Kindle edition lists 1 September 2026 as its publication date.

Questions about Dust on the River

Is this the Dust on the River game?

No. This is the novel based on the game. The game has a separate page, play destinations and platform information.
